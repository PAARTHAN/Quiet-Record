# 🎥 Demo Script: Quiet Record
**Project:** Quiet Record (Digital Legacy System)  
**Total Estimated Time:** 2:30 – 3:00 Minutes

---

## 🎙️ The Narrative Script

### **Part 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:30)**
**Speaker:**  
"Think about your financial life for a moment. Your bank accounts, your stock portfolio, the money people owe you, or even that one private note you want your family to see. 

Now, ask yourself: if something happened to you tomorrow, would your loved ones even know where to start? 

Most digital legacies are lost because they are invisible. We’re changing that. Welcome to **Quiet Record**—the digital legacy system that speaks for you, when you no longer can."

---

### **Part 2: The Vault (0:30 - 1:15)**
**Speaker:**  
"Quiet Record isn't just another password manager. It’s a secure, centralized vault for your entire financial footprint. 

From debts and insurance policies to properties and stocks—you can organize everything in seconds. Each entry allows you to add specific details, contact information for counterparties, and the exact amounts involved. 

Instead of leaving behind a mountain of paperwork, you’re building a clean, searchable, and organized digital map of your life's work. And the best part? It's private, encrypted, and accessible only to you... until the moment it's needed."

---

### **Part 3: The Trusted Circle (1:15 - 1:45)**
**Speaker:**  
"Information is useless if it doesn't reach the right people. In the Contacts section, you define your 'Trusted Circle.' These are the people who will receive your legacy report if the unthinkable happens. 

You can manage your beneficiaries with ease, ensuring that only the people you choose—and verify via their phone numbers and emails—are granted access to your information."

---

### **Part 4: The Innovation - The Safety Trigger (1:45 - 2:30)**
**Speaker:**  
"But how does the system know when to act? This is the heart of Quiet Record: **The Safety Trigger**. 

Most legacy systems require someone to 'claim' your data—which is slow and bureaucratic. Quiet Record flips the script. You set a custom inactivity timer. As long as you’re active, you simply click 'Check In' once in a while to reset the clock.

If that timer ever hits zero, it means you haven’t checked in. The system immediately goes to work. It triggers our backend engine to compile every record you’ve saved into a comprehensive **Legacy Report**."

---

### **Part 5: The Climax & Closing (2:30 - End)**
**Speaker:**  
"Within seconds of a trigger, your trusted contacts receive a secure SMS notification and an encrypted link to your full report. No lawyers, no long searches through old emails—just the information they need to manage your estate, exactly as you intended.

Quiet Record gives you the power to organize your present and protect your family's future. It’s more than just a tool; it’s peace of mind, automated.

Build your legacy today at Quiet Record."

## ⚙️ Technical Highlights (Optional Add-on)
If you want to mention the technology behind the project:
- **Backend**: Built with **FastAPI** for high-performance asynchronous processing.
- **Frontend**: A modern, responsive **React** application with a premium UI.
- **Automation**: Custom-built **Trigger Engine** that monitors user activity in real-time.
- **Notifications**: Integrated with **Twilio SMS** for instant emergency alerts.
- **Reporting**: Dynamic **Report Builder** that generates secure legacy documents on-the-fly.
